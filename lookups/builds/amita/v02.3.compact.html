AMITA
The Swamp Witch
Resident Overview

Amita is an excitable and highly curious mutant Cybunny residing in the Haunted Woods swamp. Though enthusiastic in both temperament and magical study, she is known to experiment first and evaluate the consequences second.

Resident Classification
Species: Mutant Cybunny
Residence: Haunted Woods Swamp
Occupation: Self-appointed Witch
Specialty: Experimental Witchcraft
Disposition: Social, impulsive, unpredictable
VISITOR ADVISORY

Visitors are advised against consuming unidentified potions, volunteering for experimental spellwork, or disturbing active magical procedures.

The subject's enthusiasm should not be mistaken for evidence of competence.
